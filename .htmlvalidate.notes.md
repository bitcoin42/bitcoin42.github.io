# html-validate rule exceptions

- **`no-inline-style`** — the timeline and flow diagrams position markers with
  `style="left:33%"` style attributes. Those are data, not presentation choices, and moving them
  into the stylesheet would mean generating one class per position. The remaining handful
  (`font-size` on the `%` glyph in fee tiles, a few `margin-top`s) are local typographic
  adjustments. Nothing here affects theming, which is entirely token-driven in `assets/site.css`.
- **`prefer-native-element`** — the scrollable comparison table uses `role="region"` on its
  wrapper. `<section>` would be semantically wrong for a scroll container; `role="region"` plus a
  label plus `tabindex="0"` is the WAI-ARIA pattern for keyboard-reachable scrollable content.
- **`void-style` / `attribute-boolean-style` / `no-trailing-whitespace` / `long-title`** —
  formatting preferences that do not affect correctness or accessibility.
